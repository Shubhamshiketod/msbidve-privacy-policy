Privacy Policy — M.S. Bidve Attendance

Last updated: 27 September 2026

M.S. Bidve Attendance ("the App") is provided by M.S. Bidve Engineering College, Latur, for use by its own students, faculty, HODs and Principal to access the college's attendance and academic management system. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

1. What the App is

The App is a mobile companion to the college's own web-based attendance system. It signs you in to that system and shows you the same pages you would see on the college's website, inside the app. The App itself does not run a separate service or database — every record you see is stored and managed on the college's own server.

2. Information collected

Depending on your role (student, faculty, HOD or Principal), the college's system may hold:

  • Your login identifier (email or mobile number) and password, used only to sign you in
  • Your name, roll number / employee details, department, class and academic year
  • Attendance records, timetables, lecture and syllabus records
  • Contact numbers (yours, or — for a faculty/mentor account — a student's or parent's) used for attendance follow-up and calling features
  • Leave applications, gate passes, marks, and other academic records the college's system tracks

This information is entered and stored by the college as part of running its ERP system, and existed before and independently of this App. The App is simply a way to view and update it from a phone.

3. Information stored on your device

If you turn on "Remember me" at login, the App stores an encrypted copy of your session (not your password) directly on your device, protected by a key created inside the Android Keystore. This key cannot be extracted from the device, so the stored session is unreadable anywhere else — including by us. It is automatically deleted when you log out, when it expires, or if it can ever fail to decrypt (for example, if the app's data is copied to another phone). The App never stores your password on the device.

4. Permissions the App asks for, and why

  • Internet / network state — required to communicate with the college's server. The App does not work offline.
  • Phone (Call) — used only when you tap a parent's or student's phone number shown inside the App (for example, on a calling/follow-up screen), so the call is placed directly instead of just opening the dialer. The App does not access your call log or make calls on its own.
  • Storage (Android 9 and earlier only) — used only to save a report (PDF or Excel) you choose to download, such as an attendance sheet. Newer Android versions do not need this permission for the same feature.
  • File access — when a page inside the App asks you to choose a file (for example, uploading a photo or a document), the App opens the standard Android file/photo picker. The App only receives the specific file you pick.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with advertisers or data brokers. The App contains no advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics or tracking libraries. Data you enter is transmitted only to the college's own server over an encrypted connection.

6. Data retention and deletion

Academic and attendance records are retained by the college for as long as required for academic and administrative purposes, in line with the institution's own record-keeping policies. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, please contact the college using the details below — the App itself does not provide a self-service account-deletion option, since accounts are managed by the college.

7. Children's privacy

Some users of the college's system (students) may be minors. The App does not collect any information about students beyond what the college's own academic and attendance records already contain, and does not knowingly collect additional personal information from children beyond this academic context.

8. Security

Data in transit between the App and the college's server is encrypted. The one piece of data the App stores locally (an optional "Remember me" session) is encrypted at rest using hardware-backed Android Keystore encryption, as described in Section 3.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the App changes. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
